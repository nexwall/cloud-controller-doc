# Signing in and the interface

1. Open the address of your controller (for example `https://your-company.painel.nexwall.com.br`).
2. Sign in with your **e-mail and password** (the same as in the Partner Portal). If two-step verification is enabled for your user, enter the code from your authenticator app. If you forgot the password, click **Forgot password**: a code is sent to your e-mail and you choose a new one.
3. You land on the **Firewall manager**.

From the Partner Portal you can skip the sign-in: the **Access controller** (on the customer's page) or **Open in controller** (in the Firewalls list) button signs you in automatically for a short time.

<Shots items={[{"name": "login", "caption": "The sign-in page of the controller."}]} />

The left menu is organized in sections: **Firewalls management** (Firewalls and Firewall groups), **Insights** (Reports), **Access** (administrators only) and **Help** (Support). The top bar has the language, the help button and the user menu, with **Change password**, the theme, About and Sign out: **the help button opens the page of this guide that explains the screen you are on**.

You can change the interface language with the language icon, and your password in [Change password](../administration/account-settings.md).
