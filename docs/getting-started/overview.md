# Overview

The Nexwall Cloud Controller lets you manage many Nexwall firewalls from one web page, without opening a port on any customer network. Each firewall keeps a secure VPN connection to the controller; through it you can see whether the firewall is online, open its own interface, open a terminal, update it and collect reports.

What you can do:

- **Firewall manager**: see all your firewalls, their version and whether they are connected, and open each one.
- **Firewall groups**: group firewalls (for example by customer) and decide which users can manage which groups.
- **Reports**: generate executive PDF reports for one firewall or a whole group.
- **Users and account**: controller users, your password, 2FA and SSH key.

Every customer has its own controller, separated from the others. If you are a reseller or distributor you normally reach it from the Nexwall Partner Portal with the **Open in controller** button, without typing another password.
